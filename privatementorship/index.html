What we work on

The mentorship follows an identity framework of around fifty dimensions, covering your personal identity, your professional identity and your personal characteristics. We don't go through them as a checklist: we start where it matters most to you, and the rest follows.

  • Self-understanding: what you value, what you want, and how you see yourself.
  • Dress and colour: you show me your outfits and I give you direct feedback, including which colours suit you and whether your style fits your profession and your personal brand.
  • Presence and habits: how you carry yourself, and the routines that shape it.
  • Relationships: how you relate to men, and the kind of man you attract.

What a session is like

Each session combines three things: inquiry, where we explore a question about you; teaching, where I share what I know about the subject; and direct feedback, where I tell you honestly what I see.

Every session ends with something concrete to practise before the next one: a habit to try, an outfit to put together, a conversation to have differently. I follow up on it the following week. Change happens between the sessions as much as during them.

You keep the decision at every step. What I offer is careful attention, honest feedback and a structure to think and act within, not a promise of particular outcomes.

Questions and answers

Why are the first three sessions free?

Because this is personal work, and you should know what it feels like before you commit to it. The free sessions are real sessions, not a sales pitch. If it doesn't feel right, we stop there and you owe nothing.

How many sessions will I need?

It depends on you. Most women reach the point where they no longer need me after {{ site.data.settings.typical_sessions }} sessions. The fee is the same either way.

How are sessions held?

Online, by video, one-to-one, usually once a week. We agree on a time that suits us both.

Which languages can we use?

English, French, Swedish or German, whichever is most natural to you.

Is this therapy?

No. It is mentorship: education, feedback and practice. If you are going through a mental health difficulty, please seek support from a qualified professional.

How do I begin?

Write to me by email or WhatsApp and answer three short questions. See the questions.
