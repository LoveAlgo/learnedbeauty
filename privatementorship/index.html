What we work on

Before our first session, you receive the identity form: 58 dimensions in seven categories, from your origins to where you are going in life. You fill it in as far as you wish and send it back, so that I can see who you are and what you would like to work on.

We start where it matters most to you, then work our way through the list. Along the way, I keep the whole picture in view: where one part of your life contradicts another, I point it out, as well as topics you may be tempted to avoid. Some dimensions involve concepts I explain during our sessions, from archetypes to your colour palette.

A few examples of what this covers:

  • Self-understanding: what you value, what you want, and how you see yourself.
  • Dress and colour: you show me your outfits and I give you direct feedback, including which colours suit you and whether your style fits your profession and your personal brand.
  • Presence and habits: how you carry yourself, and the routines that shape it.
  • Relationships: how you relate to men, and the kind of men you attract.

What a session is like

Each session focuses on one or a few dimensions from your identity form, usually those you have marked W. We begin with what happened since the last session, then go deeper.

A session combines three things: inquiry, where we explore what is true for you now and what you want instead; teaching, where I explain the concepts behind a dimension, such as archetypes or your colour palette; and direct feedback, where I tell you honestly what I see, including on the outfits you show me.

Every session ends with something concrete to practise before the next one: a habit to try, an outfit to put together, a conversation to have differently. I follow up on it the following week. Your form evolves with you: the “New identity” column fills in, and dimensions marked W gradually become F.

You keep the decision at every step. What I offer is careful attention, honest feedback and a structure to think and act within, not a promise of particular outcomes.

Questions and answers

Why are the first three sessions free?

Because this is personal work, and you should know what it feels like before you commit to it. The free sessions are real sessions, not a sales pitch. If it doesn't feel right, we stop there and you owe nothing.

How many sessions will I need?

It depends on you. Most women reach the point where they no longer need me after {{ site.data.settings.typical_sessions }} sessions. The fee is the same either way.

How are sessions held?

Online, by video, one-to-one, usually once a week. We agree on a time that suits us both.

Which languages can we use?

English, French, German, or Swedish, whichever is most natural to you.

Is this therapy?

No. It is mentorship: education, feedback and practice. If you are going through a mental health difficulty, please seek support from a qualified professional.

How do I begin?

Answer three reflection questions and send them by email or WhatsApp. See the application.
