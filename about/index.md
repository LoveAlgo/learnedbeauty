For as long as I can remember, I (Henrik) have been quietly devoted to the question of beauty — especially feminine beauty — and with the question of what helps a woman truly flourish.

At some point, this curiosity led me to study how professional stylists work. I expected to discover refined systems, deep understanding, and impeccable taste. Instead, what I found surprised me.

Most stylists did not reflect the depth of system or discernment I had expected. More striking still, I noticed a pattern: **women often gave better style advice to men than to each other — and almost no men were helping women cultivate beauty from a masculine point of view.**

That observation stayed with me.

At first, I dismissed it.  
A familiar inner voice spoke up:  
“Who are you, as a man, to advise women on beauty?”

After all, I don’t teach makeup techniques or eyelash tricks. I don’t lecture on dress cuts or bra sizes. I have never worn high heels — and never will.

And yet, that voice was missing something essential.

Beauty is not created by techniques alone.  
**It is received**.

What I bring is not cosmetic instruction, but perception: the perspective of a man who genuinely admires women, who notices presence, harmony, playfulness, elegance — and who understands how these qualities are felt on the other side.

For a long time, I stayed silent.  
Until I realized that this perspective is precisely what is missing.

I grew up in Geneva, surrounded by European standards of taste and refinement. One of my first summer jobs, at sixteen, was at Harry Winston — an early immersion in craftsmanship, elegance, and restraint.

Alongside this, I am a researcher by nature. I have studied thousands of images, read hundreds of books, explored psychology, self-development, aesthetics, and teaching. I am an accredited adult educator, trained not only to understand, but to transmit insight with care.

So, no — I am not a woman.  
But I am a man who loves women, studies beauty seriously, and understands what awakens admiration.

And I believe women deserve access to that perspective.

Learned Beauty was born from this realization.

## Who is Henrik

Henrik Hillerström is a multidisciplinary entrepreneur and thinker with a lifelong dedication to beauty, intelligence, and human flourishing. Born in Australia to Swedish parents, raised in Geneva, and now based in the south of France, he brings a cultivated global perspective shaped by life across nine countries and dozens of homes.

His education spans business administration (MBA, University of St. Gallen), urban and regional planning, architecture, and adult education. Professionally, he has worked in finance, entrepreneurship, research, and in the design of compatibility systems for relationships and place-based living.

A polyglot fluent in Swedish, French, English, and German (plus good understanding of several more languages), Henrik moves comfortably across cultures, disciplines, and social environments.

What unifies his work is a simple conviction:  
**beauty makes life worth living.**

With Learned Beauty, he integrates psychology, aesthetics, attraction dynamics, and cultivated taste into one elegant system: helping women become more radiant, confident, and quietly compelling through a masculine perspective grounded in good taste.
